## AI Tool: ChatGPT

### Blueprint and Project Planning

**How AI was used:**
- Brainstormed and refined the idea of a dealership parking occupancy detector.
- Helped narrow the project to a realistic Tier 1 scope.
- Helped distinguish the initial parking occupancy application from the future goal of tracking changes in vehicle inventory locations.
- Helped develop the proposed computer vision workflow using YOLO11, Python, OpenCV, and Google Colab.
- Helped plan a Region of Interest containing approximately 10–20 parking spaces.
- Helped compare possible camera viewpoints and plan the use of a consistent elevated seventh-floor viewpoint for image collection.
- Helped develop the data collection strategy and proposed dataset size.
- Helped define measurable project success metrics.
- Helped identify technical risks and possible backup plans.
- Helped organize the project milestone plan according to the course requirements.
- Helped draft and organize sections of the GitHub README and Midterm Blueprint.

### Student Contributions and Decisions

The project idea is based on a real vehicle inventory problem I have observed at my workplace. I selected the dealership parking lot as the application environment and provided information about how vehicle locations are currently recorded and how vehicles may become difficult to locate when they are moved without their new location being reported.

I also selected and photographed the proposed parking area, tested different elevated camera positions, and chose the seventh-floor viewpoint because it provides a clearer aerial view of the parking spaces. I reviewed the AI-generated suggestions and made the final decisions regarding the project scope, application, camera position, and future direction.

### Verification

AI-generated recommendations will be tested during development rather than assumed to be correct. The YOLO11 vehicle detections, parking-space occupancy predictions, accuracy measurements, and processing-time measurements will be produced and evaluated using the actual VisionLot implementation and self-collected parking-lot images.
